## The picks

### 钻石 荷花 (Hehua), hard box. China Tobacco Hebei.

- **The pack:** currently the most popular full-size pack on yanyue; launched as a New Year special. Three bands: a green lid with a lace-like repeat of white dotted lotus-petal outlines, a small red plaque with three gold stars, vertical gold calligraphy and a bold red title outlined in white; a pale cream band with a painted pink lotus and a dark leaf (gongbi style); a solid green base. Thin silver rules between the bands.
- **Recognizable:** leaf green with a pale cream middle band; a soft pink flower on cream; the dotted-outline petal repeat on the lid.
- **Palette:** green `#1d5f25` (shade), `#2c773b`, `#3f8358` (lit); cream band `#d0c5a2`; lotus pink ≈`#e8a9bb`; red title and plaque `#cf5349`.
- **The report's sketch:** a green masthead filled with a dotted petal-outline repeat (its own petal), a cream reading column, a green footer; the site name heavy and outlined, red on green with a white keyline, like the title; silver-grey small caps; thin silver double rules between bands.
- **Leave out:** the lotus painting and the leaf (the figure), the starred plaque (the brand plaque), the calligraphy and the title.
- **Search:** 钻石 荷花 烟盒 · 荷花烟 实拍 · 钻石(荷花). yanyue product 2792 (gallery, 345 photos): <https://www.yanyue.cn/product/2792>, <https://www.yanyue.cn/gallery/2792>

### 芙蓉王 (Furongwang). China Tobacco Hunan.

- **The pack:** the premium Hunan brand. The hard box: a pale cool silver-mint board, a gold frame all the way round, a small crown over an italic "Furongwang" script, one large embossed gold medallion in the middle. **The 软蓝 soft-blue variant** (the owner's pick) puts the same crown and medallion on a dark violet-navy with a diamond micro-texture, a gold rule under the lid, and a green tab with gold stars at the top.
- **Recognizable:** one central gold medallion on an otherwise empty face; the gold frame (hard box); italic Latin script under a small crown.
- **Palette:** 软蓝 navy `#272244`, 软蓝 gold `#e8ad38`; the hard box's silver-mint `#d8e1dd`, gold `#d0b75f`–`#ab8a3b`, highlight `#efe7a0`.
- **The report's sketch:** the navy with a faint diamond lattice as its own theme; the medallion's outline is generic, what's inside it is the mark.
- **Leave out:** the crown, the flower in the medallion, the script name.
- **Search:** 芙蓉王 硬 烟盒 · 芙蓉王(软蓝) · 芙蓉王 实拍. yanyue product 43 (hard), 41 (软蓝): <https://www.yanyue.cn/product/41>

### 黄山 新制皖烟 (Huangshan). China Tobacco Anhui. Laser.

- **The pack:** the whole face is holographic gold board. A fine triangle-lattice micro-emboss throws soft rainbow bands (pink, then green, then cyan) across a gold base as the pack tilts. A round medallion in the centre uses a different, cooler hologram, a faceted crystal pattern in silver-blue, under a red emblem. The wordmark and text are red.
- **Recognizable:** an all-over gold hologram with one wide rainbow sheen that sweeps as it tilts; the fine triangular lattice; a circular "crystal" hologram that sparkles differently from the field.
- **Palette:** gold base ≈`#d9c27a`; specular `#e5e4df`; pink sheen `#ba9c90`; green-gold sheen `#cbc5af`; cyan sheen `#74afb7`; red text ≈`#c8323c`.
- **The report's sketch:** gold edges with a rainbow sheen layer whose angle follows the pointer or scroll; red text on a calm, solid pale-gold reading column with no sheen behind it; a circular badge with its own cooler shimmer.
- **Leave out:** the red emblem and the wordmark. Keep the lattice faint (pattern glare), the sheen off the article, and everything still under reduced motion.
- **Search:** 黄山 新制皖烟 · 黄山(新制皖烟) 镭射 · 黄山 金皖烟 (the gold sibling). yanyue product 633: <https://www.yanyue.cn/product/633>
